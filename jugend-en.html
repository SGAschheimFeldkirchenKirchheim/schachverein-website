Questions from parents

Does my child need any previous knowledge?

No. Beginners learn the rules in training. More advanced players continue training at their level.

Do you have to sign up?

No, just drop by on a Friday at 6 pm. If you like, let us know beforehand via the contact form.

What does it cost?

Trying it out is free and without obligation. Membership for children and pupils costs €24 per year (membership form as PDF).

Does my child have to play in a team?

No, it's voluntary. Anyone who enjoys competition can play in one of our youth teams or at youth tournaments.
